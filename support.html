How it works

Filing a complaint is quick. Here's what to expect after you submit:

  • 1
    Submit your complaint Fill in the form with your details and a description of the issue.
  • 2
    Account created automatically We'll create a support account and email your login credentials.
  • 3
    Access your dashboard Log in to your account to view status, add details, and communicate with our team.
  • 4
    Resolution Our team will review and work to resolve your complaint within 3–5 business days.
